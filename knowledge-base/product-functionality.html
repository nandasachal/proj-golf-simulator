Names, scoring and the leaderboard

Golf Swing Simulator · as of 2026-11-22

Playing a game

Choose Practice or Game at the bottom of the shot panel. A game is 5 targets with one shot at each. Switch between Easy, Medium and Hard at the top of the game card (on a phone, the menu in the game row). The (i) beside the Practice and Game switch explains them. Practice has no targets and is never scored.

DifficultyThe targetClub and shotTo win
EasyThe circle at the foot of a beam. The ball has to touch it: landing, bouncing, rolling through or stopping.Any club, any shot.3 of 5
MediumThe same circle on the ground.The target names a club and a shape. Only that club and that shape count.3 of 5
HardA small spinning sphere in the air. The ball has to fly through it.The target names a club and a shape. Only that club and that shape count.4 of 5

In Medium and Hard the game card shows the club and shape under the target's distance, for example "7 iron · Draw". The shape is the way the ball curves, wherever it starts:

ShapeWhat the ball doesHow to hit it
DrawCurves left (right if you are left-handed).Club face closed to the club path.
FadeCurves right (left if you are left-handed).Club face open to the club path.
StraightDoes not curve.Club face the same as the club path.

The named club is put in your hands for you. The combinations are ones the club can really play, but not always its usual shot: you may be asked for a soft driver to 200 yd or a full 7 iron to 200 yd. Every target can be reached with whole numbers on club face, club path, aim and swing speed, leaving attack angle, loft and strike alone. If the ball reaches the target with a different club or shape it is a miss, and the game card says which ("wrong club", or "a fade, not a draw").

Every mode shows the same seven controls: club face, club path, strike, aim, attack angle, loft and swing speed. On a laptop each has a ruler to drag. The game card shows which target you are on and how many more hits you need.

In Easy the club is chosen for you. Each new target puts the club whose standard straight shot stops nearest the target's distance in your hands. You can still pick a club yourself from the club button on the shot panel; it stays for the target in front of you, and the next target picks again. To always pick your own club, turn off Auto club at the top of Settings. The choice is remembered on this device. With Auto club off, targets are ones the club in your hands can reach. Auto club makes no difference in Medium and Hard, where the target names the club. Practice never changes your club.

On your first visit the Hit button pulses until you take a shot, and your very first target is one a straight swing reaches without changing anything. After a missed target the card gives one tip on what to change, for example less club face, or more swing speed.

A won game sets off confetti and a short sound, bigger for 5 of 5. If your device is set to reduce motion, you get the sound without the confetti.

After a shot, its numbers (total, carry, run, offline, apex) show in a bar at the bottom, with the shot's line on the range. Once the next target appears they stay for 5 seconds, then clear; a thin bar under the numbers shrinks to show the time left. The x on the corner of the bar clears them straight away. In Practice they stay until you hit again or press the x.

Saving a name

You can play straight away without signing up. When a game finishes, the game card asks whether to save it to the leaderboard. You can also do this at any time from the bottom of the leaderboard, behind the trophy button. There are three choices:

Once a name is saved or you are signed in, a small circle with two initials sits on the top right corner of the game card. After signing in with Google they are the initials of your first and last name; otherwise they are two letters from your leaderboard name.

ChoiceWhat you get
Name onlyType a name and press Save. No password. The name stays on this device.
AccountContinue with Google, or a name and a password of 8 or more characters. Log in the same way on any device and your name and scores come with you. The first time you use Google you pick the name shown on the leaderboard; your Google name and email are not shown or kept.
Not nowNothing is saved. You are asked again after the next game.
  • Names are 2 to 16 characters and unique regardless of capitals. The first person to save a name owns it.
  • There is no email. Your browser remembers you until you log out.
  • The game you just finished is saved with the name. Games before it are not.
  • A name saved without a password can become an account later: open the leaderboard on the device that has it and continue with Google or add a password.
  • A forgotten password cannot be reset, because there is no email to send a reset to. Signing in with Google has no password to forget.

To use your account on a new device, choose Account, then "Have an account? Log in", and enter the name and password, or continue with Google. Log out from the bottom of the leaderboard.

What the site stores about you is set out on the Privacy page, linked from Settings and from the save form.

How you are scored

RuleWhat it means
Hit %Targets hit divided by targets attempted, across every game you have played in that mode.
Every shot countsA shot is counted the moment it lands. Restarting a game that started badly does not remove its shots.
15 targets to be rankedUntil you have attempted 15 targets (three games) you are listed as "warming up" and have no rank.
Separate boardsEasy, Medium and Hard are ranked separately. Hard was called Advanced until 2026-11-22 and did not name a club or shape; shots from then are still on its board.
TiesThe player who has attempted more targets ranks higher.

The leaderboard

Open it with the trophy button next to Settings. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard, and This week or All time. The week starts on Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Each row shows rank, name, hit %, games played and best streak (the longest run of hits in a row). The top 20 are listed. Once you have played in that mode and period, your own row is always shown and highlighted; if you are outside the top 20 it appears below the list with the players just above and below you.

After each finished game the game card shows where you stand: your rank and hit %, or how many more targets you need to be ranked. When someone is just ahead of you it also says how many more hits would pass them.

When something goes wrong

What you seeWhy
"That name is taken"Someone already saved it. Pick another, or log in if it is your account.
"That name and password don't match"The name or password was mistyped, or the name was saved without a password.
"Leaderboard is unavailable right now"The leaderboard service could not be reached. The game still plays; your shots are kept on your device and sent when it is back.
"Too many tries"Too many names or passwords were tried in a short time. Wait a minute.
Your name is goneThe browser's data was cleared, or this is a different device. Log in if you made an account; a name saved without a password cannot be brought back.

Limits

  • Scores are worked out in your browser. The board is meant for friends, not for strangers competing for prizes.
  • Offensive names are refused, and the site owner can remove a player.
