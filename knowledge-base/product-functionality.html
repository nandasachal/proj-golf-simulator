Names, scoring and the leaderboard

Golf Swing Simulator · as of 2026-11-21

Playing a game

Choose Practice or Game at the bottom of the shot panel. A game is 5 targets with one shot at each. Switch between Normal and Advanced at the top of the game card. In Normal the target is the circle at the foot of a beam and 3 hits win. In Advanced the target is a small spinning sphere in the air and 4 hits win. The (i) on each button says what it is. Practice has no targets and is never scored.

Normal shows three controls: club face, club path and swing speed. Advanced and Practice show all seven. The game card shows which target you are on and how many more hits you need.

On your first visit the Hit button pulses until you take a shot, and your very first target is one a straight swing reaches without changing anything. After a missed target the card gives one tip on what to change, for example less club face, or more swing speed.

A won game sets off confetti and a short sound, bigger for 5 of 5. If your device is set to reduce motion, you get the sound without the confetti.

Saving a name

You can play straight away without signing up. When a game finishes, the game card asks whether to save it to the leaderboard. Type a name and press Save. You can also save a name at any time from the bottom of the leaderboard, behind the trophy button.

  • Names are 2 to 16 characters and unique regardless of capitals. The first person to save a name owns it.
  • There is no email or password. Your browser remembers you.
  • The game you just finished is saved with the name. Games before it are not.
  • You are shown a recovery code once. Write it down: it is the only way to use your name on another phone or browser, or after clearing your browser data.

To use your name on a new device, finish a game there, choose "Already have a name? Use recovery code", and enter the name and the code. Your name then works on both devices.

How you are scored

RuleWhat it means
Hit %Targets hit divided by targets attempted, across every game you have played in that mode.
Every shot countsA shot is counted the moment it lands. Restarting a game that started badly does not remove its shots.
15 targets to be rankedUntil you have attempted 15 targets (three games) you are listed as "warming up" and have no rank.
Separate boardsNormal and Advanced are ranked separately.
TiesThe player who has attempted more targets ranks higher.

The leaderboard

Open it with the trophy button next to Settings. Choose Normal or Advanced, and This week or All time. The week starts on Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Each row shows rank, name, hit %, games played and best streak (the longest run of hits in a row). The top 20 are listed. Once you have played in that mode and period, your own row is always shown and highlighted; if you are outside the top 20 it appears below the list with the players just above and below you.

After each finished game the game card shows where you stand: your rank and hit %, or how many more targets you need to be ranked. When someone is just ahead of you it also says how many more hits would pass them.

When something goes wrong

What you seeWhy
"That name is taken"Someone already saved it. Pick another, or use your recovery code if it is yours.
"That name and code don't match"The recovery code or name was mistyped.
"Leaderboard is unavailable right now"The leaderboard service could not be reached. The game still plays; your shots are kept on your device and sent when it is back.
"Too many tries"Too many names or codes were tried in a short time. Wait a minute.
Your name is goneThe browser's data was cleared, or this is a different device. Use your recovery code.

Limits

  • Scores are worked out in your browser. The board is meant for friends, not for strangers competing for prizes.
  • Offensive names are refused, and the site owner can remove a player.
