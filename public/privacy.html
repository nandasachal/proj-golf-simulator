Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Golf Swing Simulator (golfswingsimulator.com) is a free hobby site run by one individual in California, USA ("I"). I decide how data on this site is used. Contact: brownwaldo94@gmail.com.

Short version

You can play without giving me anything. If you save a name or create an account, I store it so the leaderboard works. I don't sell data, run ads, send marketing, or collect email addresses.

What I collect

  • Leaderboard name (optional). 2 to 16 characters. It is public, shown with your hit %, games played and best streak. Don't use your real name or anything private.
  • Account (optional). Either a name and password, or Sign in with Google. Passwords are stored only as a salted scrypt hash, so I can't read them. With Google, your browser gets a token from Google, and my server verifies it and stores only Google's opaque user ID. The token also contains your email, name and picture; I don't store or use them. I request only basic sign-in and get no access to your Gmail, Drive or other Google data. My use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Shots (saved players only). Game id, mode, club, target distance, hit or miss, and time. They are used to rank the public leaderboard.
  • Analytics. I use PostHog to count named events (such as starting a game), capture errors, and see device and browser type. It is anonymous until you save a name, then it is linked to your internal player ID, not your name. On a browser's first visit I also record a session replay of how the page is used, with all text and form inputs masked.
  • Technical data. Your IP address reaches my host (Render) and PostHog as part of every request. The leaderboard service uses IP addresses in memory to limit abuse. I don't store them in my database.

Your device

The site itself sets no cookies. It uses your browser's localStorage for a secret key that identifies your saved player on that device (stored hashed on my server), shots waiting to be sent, your settings, a first-visit flag, the analytics ID, and a marker that keeps my own devices out of the analytics. Clearing site data logs that device out. If you open the account options, Google's sign-in button is loaded from Google, which may set its own cookies.

Why I use it

To run the game, leaderboard and accounts (providing the service you asked for), and to fix bugs and improve the game (my legitimate interest, which you can object to).

Who receives it

Render (hosting, leaderboard service and database, US), PostHog (analytics, US), and Google (only when you open the account options or use Sign in with Google; Google handles that sign-in under its own privacy policy). Render and PostHog process data on my behalf. I don't sell data or share it for advertising. I would disclose data only if the law requires it.

Where it is processed

Data is processed in the United States by the providers above, under their data processing terms.

How long I keep it

Saved players and their shots are kept until you ask me to delete them or the leaderboard closes. Analytics events and replays are kept by PostHog for its standard retention periods and then deleted.

Your rights

Email brownwaldo94@gmail.com to see, correct or delete your data. I reply within 30 days. I may ask you to prove you control the account, for example by logging in. Deletion removes your player, your shots and your analytics profile. There is no password reset, because I hold no email address for you, so keep your password.

If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the rights of restriction, portability and objection, and the right to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in California, I am not a business covered by the CCPA, but I give you the same access and deletion rights, I don't sell or share your data, and I won't treat you differently for asking. No decisions about you are made automatically.

Children

The site is not directed at children. Please don't create an account if you are under 16. If I learn a child's data is stored, I will delete it.

Do Not Track

The site does not respond to Do Not Track signals. It collects no cross-site data.

Changes

I will update this page and its date when practices change.
